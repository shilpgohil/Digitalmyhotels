1. Executive Summary
DigitalMyHotels is a multi-tenant SaaS hotel management platform for managing hotel properties, rooms, reservations, guests, check-in/check-out, payments, invoices, GST, expenses, housekeeping, reports, staff permissions and hotel subscriptions. The platform contains a Hotel Partner Portal and a Main Admin/Super Admin Portal. Each hotel must have strict tenant-level data isolation.
2. Source Requirements Retained
The uploaded requirements define the two major portals, role hierarchy, partner modules, dashboard, hotel management, room management, booking, guest management, check-in, checkout, current guests, expenses, payments, invoices, GST, owner team management, housekeeping, reports, audit logs, notifications, Super Admin, subscriptions, database, APIs, security, business rules, edge cases, UI/UX, user flows, technology, development phases, MVP and QA. The final document retains those requirements and adds the missing hotel-operational controls identified during review.

9. Guest Check-in — Complete Workflow
Search existing booking or create a walk-in booking
Select existing guest or create a new guest
Mark Primary Guest and add accompanying guests
Verify guest details and required ID information
Select/allocate room after availability validation
Capture actual check-in date/time and expected checkout
Capture adults/children and occupancy
Calculate room rate, extra guest charges, discounts and GST
Record advance payment and/or security deposit
Select payment method and transaction reference where applicable
Calculate paid, partial and due amounts
Generate receipt and invoice as applicable
Confirm check-in and change room status to Occupied
Payment methods: Cash, UPI, Card, Bank Transfer and Other.
Early check-in must support hotel policy, grace period, optional fee and authorized approval.
10. Guest Check-out — Complete Workflow
Open active guest stay and verify room/guest details
Capture actual checkout date/time
Calculate number of nights and room charges
Add all additional hotel charges/services
Apply approved discounts and GST
Apply previous payments and security deposit
Calculate final total, paid amount, due amount and refund amount
Collect outstanding payment or process authorized due/credit case
Generate final invoice and payment receipt
Confirm checkout
Automatically set room to Cleaning Required
Record checkout in audit history
Late checkout must support grace period, late fee and authorized approval.
If a balance remains due, the system must either block checkout or allow an authorized checkout with Payment Due status, due date, reason and approving user.
Completed checkout reversal/reopen must be restricted to Owner/Manager or an explicitly authorized role and must create an audit record.

31. Critical Business Rules
1. Hotel data must be isolated between tenants.
2. Only Hotel Owner can create hotel team accounts.
3. Manager can access financial/payment/expense information.
4. Admin/Reception can manage check-in/check-out and operational room status.
5. Housekeeping can manage cleaning/maintenance/status and cannot access financial information.
6. Double booking must be prevented.
7. Occupied, blocked or maintenance rooms cannot be allocated.
8. Checkout changes room to Cleaning Required.
9. Cleaning completion changes room to Available.
10. Payment status must correctly reflect unpaid/partial/paid/refunded.
11. Invoice totals must reconcile with charge and payment records.
12. GST must be calculated by centralized rules.
13. Financial corrections must never silently overwrite history.
14. Every material financial/operational change must be audited.
15. Daily closing corrections require authorization.
16. Expired hotels follow defined subscription restrictions.

35. Recommended Technology Architecture
Preferred direction: Frontend React/Next.js; Backend Node.js; relational database recommended for transactional consistency and reporting. The final implementation should include secure authentication, REST APIs, object/file storage, PDF invoice generation, notification services, background jobs, logging, monitoring, backups, deployment and CI/CD.
The architecture must support multi-tenancy, transactional financial operations and horizontal growth without mixing hotel data.

41. Final Implementation Notes
This specification should be treated as the baseline for UI/UX, frontend, backend, QA and AI coding work. Before implementation, the Figma prototype should be compared screen-by-screen with this specification. Where Figma and the functional specification differ, the product owner should explicitly approve the final behavior. Financial, room-allocation and tenant-isolation rules must be implemented as backend-enforced rules, not only UI restrictions.
